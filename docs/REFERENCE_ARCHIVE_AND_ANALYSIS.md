## 4. Universal Anti-Chaos Rules Extracted Across All 25 Sites
1. **One Anchor Per Viewport:** Never stack competing cards, badges, and widgets in the hero. Commit to one dominant 3D object, portrait, or typographic lockup.
2. **60%+ Intentional Negative Space:** Let the background (`#08080a`, `#141414`, `#ffffff`, or `#ebe8e2`) breathe so the focal piece feels expensive.
3. **Micro-Metadata Discipline:** Corner labels stay `10px–11px` monospace with wide tracking (`0.14em–0.18em`), low-opacity secondary ink, and zero unnecessary borders.
4. **Audio/Entry Rituals:** Gate sound-driven worlds with a crisp, optional entry (`ENTER WITH SOUND ›` / `ENTER WITHOUT SOUND` or `Click to power on`).
5. **GPU-First Motion:** Prefer shader/transform/opacity choreography over CPU-heavy DOM/SVG filters so transitions stay locked at 60fps on both desktop and mobile.
