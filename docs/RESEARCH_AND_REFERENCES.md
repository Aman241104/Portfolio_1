Key adapted principles applied in this overhaul:
1. **Museum-frame hero discipline:** Full `100svh` framing with zero below-the-fold bleed/peeking.
2. **Spatial breathing room:** Separating 3D focal accents (the anodized/chrome orb) from editorial typography (`Creative Developer`) and mobile header metadata (`AMAN®`).
3. **Uncropped case-study media:** Preserving full product UI screenshots inside case studies without destructive `object-fit: cover` edge cropping.
